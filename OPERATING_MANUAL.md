# Sanitary Pad Dispenser — Operating Manual

A simple guide to using and looking after the machine. No technical
knowledge needed.

**Service contact:** ___________________________  *(write the phone number
printed on the machine here)*

---

## 1. What the machine does

It sells one sanitary pad for **KSh 20**. A customer puts coins in, the
machine shows what they have paid, and when it reaches KSh 20 a pad drops out
for them to collect.

It takes **KSh 5, 10, 20 and 40 coins**. It does **not give change** and it
**cannot give money back** — so it only ever takes payment once a pad is on
its way, and anything a customer overpays stays on the screen as credit
toward the next pad.

---

## 2. For the customer — how to buy a pad

1. **Put a coin in the slot.** The screen shows what you have so far, e.g.
   `THAT IS KSH 5!` then `ADD KSH 15 MORE`.
2. **Keep adding coins** until you reach KSh 20. You can use any mix —
   four 5s, two 10s, one 20, or one 40.
3. When you reach KSh 20 the screen says **`KSH 20 - PAID!`** and a pad comes
   out. Take it when it says **`PLEASE COLLECT YOUR PAD`**.
4. A **KSh 40** coin buys **two pads**, one after the other.

**If you pay part and walk away**, the machine keeps asking for about two
minutes (`TIME LEFT 1:58` counting down), then clears it.

---

## 3. What the screen is saying

The screen is 2 lines. A long message moves through a few pages — wait and it
repeats. Here is what each message means.

### Normal, everything fine

| The screen shows | What it means |
|---|---|
| `PAD = KSH 20` / `INSERT A COIN - THIS MACHINE TAKES KSH 5 10 20 OR 40` | Ready and waiting. Put a coin in. |
| `THAT IS KSH 5!` / `TOTAL KSH 5` | It received your coin; this is your total so far. |
| `ADD KSH 15 MORE` / `TIME LEFT 1:58` | Not enough yet. Add the rest before the timer runs out. |
| `KSH 20 - PAID!` / `A FULL PAD!` | Paid in full. A pad is coming. |
| `KSH 40 - PAID!` / `2 PADS COMING!` | Paid for two pads. |
| `HERE IT COMES...` | The pad is being pushed out now. |
| `PLEASE COLLECT` / `YOUR PAD :)` | Take your pad. |
| `ONE MORE COMING` / `PAD 2 OF 2` | The second pad (after a KSh 40) is on its way. |
| `THANK YOU!` / `HAVE A GOOD DAY` | Finished. |
| `PLEASE WAIT` / `CREDIT KSH 5` | A coin arrived while a pad was dispensing; it is held for the next pad. |

### Things to act on

| The screen shows | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| `COIN NOT TAKEN` / `THIS MACHINE TAKES KSH 5 10 20 OR 40` | A coin it does not recognise was returned. | Use a 5, 10, 20 or 40 coin. |
| `SORRY - NO PADS` / `COIN SLOT CLOSED - PLEASE COME BACK LATER` | **Out of pads.** It is not taking money. | **Staff: refill the coil** (section 6). It starts working again by itself. |
| `SORRY - NO PADS` / `YOUR KSH 5 IS SAFE AND WAITING FOR YOU` | Out of pads, but a customer's part-payment is still saved. | Refill; the saved credit then buys a pad. |
| `PAID - NO PAD` / `HOLD THE BUTTON FOR 1 SECOND TO TRY AGAIN` | Someone paid but no pad came out. Their money is safe. | **Press and hold the front button for 1 second** to try again. |
| `SORRY - JAMMED` + a phone number | A pad did not come out after several tries. | **Staff needed** (section 7). The customer should call the number. |
| `OUT OF ORDER` / `CALL THE NUMBER ABOVE` | The machine has a fault and has shut the coin slot. **It is not taking anyone's money.** | **Staff: see section 7.** |
| `CHUTE BLOCKED` / `PLEASE CLEAR THE CHUTE THEN HOLD THE BUTTON AGAIN` | Something is in the chute, or the drop sensor is covered. | **Staff: clear the chute** and hold the button. |
| `PAD IS PAID FOR` / `PRESS THE BUTTON TO COLLECT IT` | After a power cut, a pad that was already paid for is waiting. | Press the front button (or put a coin in) to release it. |

The key thing for a customer to know: **if the screen says SORRY or OUT OF
ORDER, the machine has stopped taking money.** Nobody is charged for a pad
that does not come out.

---

## 4. Switching on

- Plug in the **12 V power adapter**. The screen lights up, shows
  `PAD DISPENSER` / `FIRMWARE v2.0` for a moment, runs a quick self-check, and
  then `PAD = KSH 20`.
- The backlight **dims after 2 minutes** with nobody using it. A coin or a
  button press wakes it. The writing is still there in the dark; this is
  normal and saves power.

If the screen stays blank, see section 8.

---

## 5. If the power goes out

**No money is ever lost to a power cut.** The machine saves what a customer has
paid, and remembers it when the power comes back:

- If they had **paid in full** for a pad that had not yet dropped, the screen
  comes back to `PAD IS PAID FOR` / `PRESS THE BUTTON TO COLLECT IT`. The
  customer presses the front button, or puts in a coin, and the pad comes out.
- If they had **part-paid** (less than KSh 20), that amount is remembered and
  the two-minute countdown starts again from when the power returns.

So a customer who was mid-payment when the power blinked does not lose their
coins — they are still on the screen.

### About the countdown

When a customer pays part of the price and stops, the machine keeps asking —
`ADD KSH 15 MORE` / `TIME LEFT 1:58` — counting down for about two minutes. If
nobody adds the rest in that time, the part-payment is cleared. (Those coins
are already in the cash box and cannot be returned, which is why the machine
only ever asks for small top-ups, never large ones, before timing out.)

---

## 6. For the attendant — refilling pads

1. Open the machine with the key.
2. **Load pads into the coil from the front (the dropping end) first**, filling
   toward the back. Keep the front slots full — that is what makes each pad
   come out quickly.
3. Close and lock the machine.
4. It returns to service on its own — `SORRY - NO PADS` changes back to
   `PAD = KSH 20` once it senses pads are loaded.

**Do not leave only one or two pads sitting at the far back of the coil.** A
pad far from the front takes a long time to wind forward and the machine may
report it as a jam. Keep the front filled.

---

## 7. When it says OUT OF ORDER or JAMMED — what to check

The machine shuts the coin slot whenever it cannot do its job, so no money is
taken while it is faulted. To bring it back:

**First, the easy fix for a jam:** open the machine, clear whatever is stuck,
then **lift the pads out of the coil and put them back**. The machine treats
that as a service visit and returns to work on its own, dispensing any pad it
owed. No tools needed.

**If that does not do it, check, in order:**

| Check | How |
|---|---|
| **Is a pad stuck in the chute?** | Look down the chute where the pad drops. Clear anything caught. |
| **Is the coil jammed?** | Turn the coil by hand (power off). It should move freely. |
| **Is the drop sensor in place?** | It sits across the chute. If its wire is knocked loose, or something is sitting in the beam, the screen says `CHECK SENSOR`. Reseat the wire and clear the chute. It fixes itself once the chute is clear. |
| **Are pads actually loaded?** | `SORRY - NO PADS` just means refill (section 6). |

**To force it back into service** after fixing the problem:
- **Hold the front button for 1 second**, or
- Lift the pads out and back (above), or
- **Press the small RESET button inside** the machine. The screen restarts.
  Any money a customer already paid is remembered and the pad is held for
  collection.

A customer's paid-for pad is **never lost** to a reset or a fault — it waits
on the meter until collected.

## 8. Quick problem guide

| Problem | Likely cause | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Screen is blank | No power, or a rare start-up hiccup | Check the 12 V adapter is plugged in. Press the small RESET button inside. If it comes up within a few seconds on its own, that is the machine recovering by itself. |
| Screen lit but the writing never changes | Rare | Press the RESET button inside. |
| Screen shows strange or jumbled characters | A rare electrical glitch | Switch the power off and on again; it clears. It has not happened since the wiring was tidied, so tell the service contact if you ever see it. |
| Coins come straight back out | Coin not recognised, or the slot is closed (out of pads / faulted) | Use a 5/10/20/40 coin. If every coin is returned, check the screen — it is probably `SORRY - NO PADS` or `OUT OF ORDER`. |
| Paid, no pad came out | A slow or stuck pad | The customer holds the front button 1 second to retry. If it keeps failing, staff clear the jam (section 7). |
| Keeps saying JAMMED after refilling | Pads loaded only at the far back | Refill from the front (section 6). |
| Machine restarted by itself | It caught an internal problem and recovered | Normal safety behaviour. If it happens often, note it and call the service contact. |

---

## 9. Good habits

- **Keep the coil filled from the front.**
- **Empty the coin box regularly.**
- Keep the chute and the drop sensor clean and clear.
- Switch the power off before unplugging the coin acceptor connector.
- If a fault keeps coming back, write down exactly what the screen said and
  call the service contact — that message is the fastest way to find the
  cause.

---

*This machine takes payment only once a pad is on its way, and can never
return coins. If a customer is ever owed a pad it could not deliver, the
money stays saved and the pad is given when the machine is working again.*
